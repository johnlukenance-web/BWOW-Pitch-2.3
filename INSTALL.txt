Bwow Pitch 2 - Windows x64 VST3 (REAPER)

1. Copy the whole folder "Bwow Pitch 2.vst3" into:
      C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
   (needs admin rights; alternatively add any folder you like under
    REAPER > Options > Preferences > Plug-ins > VST > "Add path".)
2. In REAPER: Options > Preferences > Plug-ins > VST > "Re-scan..." and choose
   "Clear cache and re-scan VST paths for new plug-ins" (or hold Ctrl and click Re-scan).
3. On the guitar DI track: FX > Add > search "Bwow Pitch 2" (VST3) and place it BEFORE the amp sim.
4. Defaults: Amount 65 cents, Decay 143 ms, Trigger Sensitivity Auto. The orange dot lights on each detected pick.
   The plug-in reports 10 ms latency to REAPER (delay compensation is automatic).
